About Holcare

Primary Healthcare Clinic

Holcare Health & Wellness Clinic is a Primary Healthcare Clinic that focuses on the prevention and treatment of chronic conditions through a multidisciplinary approach with the aid of our physicians, nutritionists, physiotherapists, health coaches, technicians, etc.

At Holcare Health & Wellness Clinic, we are dedicated to tackling chronic conditions by focusing on the root cause of diseases using the functional medicine approach. We practice healthcare and not disease care. We work with our patients to enable them to achieve a better health outcome.

Our mission is to work with our patients to help them achieve optimal health, thus realizing what it means to feel good once again.

Our Unique Position

Pioneers in Nigeria

We are the pioneers of Functional Medicine & Hyperbaric Medicine in Nigeria, Africa's most populous country with over 220 million persons, located in the heart of Nigeria's Federal Capital Territory, Abuja.

Holcare Health & Wellness Clinic was established to be a safe space where the patient's voice is heard and regarded. Thus, offering patient-centered, and not disease-centered medicine, where individuals can regain their health and find access to and benefit from medical science and information that has not yet been incorporated into conventional medical care.

Pioneers in Nigeria Abuja, FCT Patient-Centered Care

Our Founder

Dr. Etse Kelsy

Co-founder of Holcare Health & Wellness Clinic - A physician practicing Hyperbaric and Functional Medicine with certifications obtained from National Board of Diving & Hyperbaric Medical Technology (NBDHMT) and the Institute of Functional Medicine (IFM).

Her quest for answers to the epidemic of chronic diseases led her to Functional Medicine and the establishment of Holcare Health & Wellness Clinic.

Holcare offers personalized patient management using the Functional Medicine approach. Her goal is to reduce the burden of chronic diseases in her community, one patient at a time.

She prioritizes educating her community on healthy lifestyle choices to improve health-span and lifespan.

NBDHMT Certified IFM Certified Hyperbaric Medicine Functional Medicine
