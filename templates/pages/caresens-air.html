What is CareSens Air?

CareSens Air is an easy-to-use, clinically informed oxygen monitoring device designed to support patients with asthma, COPD, post-COVID recovery, and other respiratory needs. It helps you track blood oxygen saturation and pulse rate in real time, so you and your care team can act sooner.

Reliable Monitoring

Continuous oxygen data that helps detect early signs of breathing difficulty before symptoms worsen.

Patient-Friendly

Comfortable and simple to use at home, in the clinic, or during travel.

Connected Care

Easy sharing of oxygen data with your Holcare care team to improve clinical decisions.

Early Intervention

Alerts and reports that support timely action for safer breathing management.

How CareSens Air Works

1. Place and Pair

Position the CareSens Air sensor and connect it to the companion app or clinicians dashboard using Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

2. Measure Blood Oxygen

CareSens Air continuously measures SpO2 and pulse rate, giving you a clear view of oxygen levels throughout the day.

3. Review and Respond

Track trends, receive alerts for drops in oxygen saturation, and share results with your Holcare care team for prompt support.

Why CareSens Air is Necessary

Monitoring oxygen levels is essential for people with respiratory conditions, those recovering from infection, and anyone who needs extra breathing support. CareSens Air helps prevent complications by making breathing status visible and understandable.

  • Identifies low oxygen early, even before shortness of breath appears.
  • Supports safer home care and reduces unplanned clinic visits.
  • Improves clinical oversight for Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD), asthma, pneumonia, and recovery care.
  • Integrates data into Holcare's care plans to help personalize therapy and medication decisions.

Installation and Setup

Remove CareSens Air from the packaging and confirm that the sensor, charging cable, user guide, and any mounting accessories are included and undamaged.

Charge the device fully, then switch it on. Open the Holcare companion app or dashboard and follow the on-screen pairing instructions to connect CareSens Air via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

Place the sensor in the recommended position, then begin a measurement. Confirm that the readings appear correctly in the app and that oxygen levels are updating regularly.

Review the daily oxygen report and set alert thresholds for low readings. Share the report with Holcare clinicians if you need personalized guidance or therapy adjustment.
