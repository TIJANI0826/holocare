What is CareSens Air?

CareSens Air is a continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) device designed to help people living with diabetes understand their glucose levels throughout the day and night. Instead of relying only on occasional finger-prick checks, CareSens Air shows glucose trends, alerts users to highs and lows, and helps patients and clinicians make better-informed care decisions.

Continuous Glucose Insight

Regular glucose readings and trend information that help you see how food, medication, exercise, stress, and sleep affect your levels.

Patient-Friendly

A discreet wearable sensor that supports everyday use at home, work, school, or while travelling.

Connected Care

Share glucose reports with your Holcare care team so treatment plans can be reviewed with clearer evidence.

Early Intervention

Alerts and reports that support timely action when glucose levels are rising too high or dropping too low.

How CareSens Air Works

1. Apply and Pair

Apply the CareSens Air sensor as instructed and pair it with the compatible app or reader so glucose data can be displayed.

2. Track Glucose Trends

CareSens Air measures glucose in the fluid under the skin and displays readings, trend arrows, and patterns that help you understand where your glucose is heading.

3. Review and Respond

Use alerts, daily summaries, and time-in-range reports to guide safer decisions about meals, activity, medication routines, and follow-up care.

Why CareSens Air is Necessary

Glucose can change quickly and silently. CareSens Air helps make those changes visible, especially between routine finger-prick checks. With continuous glucose monitoring, patients can better understand patterns, reduce guesswork, and work with clinicians to prevent avoidable complications from high or low glucose.

  • Identifies glucose highs and lows early, including overnight changes that may otherwise be missed.
  • Supports safer day-to-day diabetes management with clearer insight into glucose patterns.
  • Helps people with Type 1 diabetes, Type 2 diabetes, gestational diabetes, or insulin therapy monitor their response to treatment.
  • Integrates glucose reports into Holcare's care plans to help personalize nutrition, activity, medication, and follow-up decisions.

Installation and Setup

Remove CareSens Air from the packaging and confirm that the sensor, applicator, user guide, and any included accessories are present and undamaged. Check the expiry date before use.

Open the compatible CareSens app or reader and follow the pairing instructions. Make sure your phone or reader is nearby and that Bluetooth is enabled where required.

Clean and dry the recommended application site, then apply the sensor according to the user guide. Confirm that readings begin after the device's startup period and follow any calibration instructions provided for your device model.

Set high and low glucose alerts based on clinical guidance. Review daily patterns, time in range, and glucose variability, then share reports with Holcare clinicians when you need personalized support.
